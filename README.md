# Aleksei Ostapolets

Economist working at the intersection of causal inference, international trade, economic history, and geospatial analysis.

My research turns large and often messy datasets into policy-relevant evidence. I work with panel data, difference-in-differences, event studies, gravity models, matching, synthetic control, and spatial data.

## Selected research

### Geopolitical fragmentation and green-technology supply chains

How geopolitical distance reshapes trade, production, and renewable-energy deployment across solar, wind, and battery value chains.

[View project](https://github.com/alexayos/geopolitical-green-tech-supply-chains)

### The 1891–1892 famine and migration under the Stolypin reforms

Historical evidence on how a severe crop shock affected migration decisions fifteen years later, once institutional barriers to resettlement were relaxed.

[View project](https://github.com/alexayos/famine-stolypin-migration)

### Nuclear infrastructure and local economies in France

A geospatial causal-inference project combining matching, event studies, difference-in-differences, and synthetic control to study nuclear-hosting departments and the Fessenheim closure.

[View project](https://github.com/alexayos/france-nuclear-local-economies)
